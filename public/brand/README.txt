OXYGEN NURSERY — BRAND ASSETS
-----------------------------
logo-full.png   Official logo, full lockup (ON monogram + OXYGEN NURSERY wordmark
                + "Green Begins With Us." tagline). Background made transparent.
logo-mark.png   Official logo, monogram only (ON + globe + tree + sprout).
                Used where the full lockup would be too wide — navbar, mobile bar.

Both are derived from the supplied artwork by removing the white background only.
The logo itself is unmodified: no recolouring, redrawing or distortion.

The mark is drawn in deep evergreen, so on dark sections the UI sets it on a soft
white tile (.brand-tile) rather than inverting it.

All paths are referenced from ONE place: src/config/businessConfig.ts -> BRAND.
Favicons (/favicon-32.png, /favicon-64.png, /apple-touch-icon.png) are generated
from the monogram; regenerate them if the logo is ever updated.
